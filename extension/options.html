Data & Privacy Disclosure

When automatic renaming, open-chat renaming/regeneration, or batch library renaming runs on https://www.perplexity.ai/*, only the opening question (up to 600 chars) and an excerpt of the first answer (up to 1,200 chars) are sent directly from your browser to the LLM API endpoint you configure above solely to generate a 3-7 word thread title. Your API key, settings, and rename history stay in chrome.storage.local on your device. Zero backend servers, analytics, or telemetry. Independent project; not affiliated with Perplexity AI.
